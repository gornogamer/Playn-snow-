## ✨ Features

1. **Interactive Winter Landscape & Physics**:
   - 960 × 640 responsive viewport with falling snow particles, aurora borealis twilight sky, and footprint trails.
   - Ballistic parabolic snowball throwing mechanics with impact particle bursts.
   - Zero-latency procedural Web Audio chimes, snow crunches, and 8-bit motifs.

2. **🎅 Santa Claus Station & Flying Reindeer Sleigh**:
   - Meet Santa Claus with an animated Santa NPC.
   - Throw snowballs at Santa for sparkle bursts and authentic procedural *"Ho Ho Ho!"* audio.
   - Equip a floppy fur Santa Hat and red winter coat on your Rare Friend.
   - Toggle **Santa's Magic Snowballs** with golden star trails and holiday bell chimes.
   - Launch Santa's Reindeer Sleigh flying across the northern sky led by glowing red-nosed Rudolph!

3. **⛄ Modular Snowman Builder**:
   - 3-tier snowman sculpting studio (Base, Torso, Head).
   - Customize with Santa Cap, Top Hat, Beanie, Carrot Nose, Twig Arms, Candy Cane buttons, and colorful scarves.
   - Placed snowmen persist across the snowfield.

4. **🎯 Snowball Target Range**:
   - Moving targets, golden bells, accuracy tracking, and streak combos.

5. **🎁 Frost Crate Depot (FriendSDK Chance Game)**:
   - Built with `@rarefriends/friendsdk` v0.1.2 chance game definitions and simulated $RF prize rewards.
